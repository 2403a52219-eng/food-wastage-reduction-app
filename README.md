# food-wastage-reduction-app

Agile Scrum simulation project for reducing food wastage by connecting restaurants with NGOs.

# Study 31 – Food Wastage Reduction App

## Product Vision
To develop a platform that connects restaurants with NGOs to donate excess food and distribute it to people in need, reducing food wastage.

## Users / Stakeholders
- Restaurants: Post details of excess food.
- NGOs: Find and collect donated food.
- Admin: Manage users and monitor donations.
